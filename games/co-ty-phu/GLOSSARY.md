# Cờ Tỷ Phú

A Turn-based Game for 2–4 seats: players roll two dice around a 40-tile board of Vietnamese
places, buy and upgrade Lots, take them from each other, and play until all but one are bankrupt.

## Board

**Tile**:
One of the 40 squares on the board, in the fixed order of the legacy board. Players see "ô".
_Avoid_: Square, cell, space

**Lot**:
A Tile that can be owned: a city, a bến xe, Điện lực or Cấp nước. Players see "đất".
_Avoid_: Property, deed, estate

**City**:
A Lot named after a Vietnamese place, the only kind of Lot that has Levels.
_Avoid_: Street, province

**Colour set**:
The three Cities that share a colour; there are eight. Owning all three is a Full set.
_Avoid_: Group, monopoly, "three adjacent tiles"

**Full set**:
A Colour set whose three Cities have one owner, which doubles their rent.
_Avoid_: Monopoly

## Owning

**Level**:
How far a City is built, from 1 to 5: Levels 1–4 are one to four houses (players see "2 nhà",
not "Nhà 2"), then Khách sạn. Buying a City builds Level 1.
_Avoid_: House count, building, upgrade (for the state)

**Hotel**:
Level 5 of a City; a Hotel can no longer be Taken over. Players see "Khách sạn".
_Avoid_: Landmark

**Takeover**:
Buying a Lot from its owner after landing on it and paying its rent, at a price set by the
rules; the owner cannot refuse. Players see "Mua lại".
_Avoid_: Steal, capture, trade

**Debt**:
A payment larger than the payer's cash. It is paid by Selling Lots to the bank, or ends in
Bankruptcy.
_Avoid_: Loan, mortgage

**Selling**:
Giving Levels or a whole Lot back to the bank for half their value; a sold Lot has no owner.
_Avoid_: Mortgage, liquidation

## Chance

**Card**:
One draw from the Cơ hội or Khí vận deck, applied as soon as it is drawn except the jail-free
Card, which is kept.
_Avoid_: Event, chance (for one draw)

**Effect**:
A lasting boon or curse on one player, counted down in that player's own turns; a player holds
at most two. Players see its icon on their player card.
_Avoid_: Buff, debuff, status

**Festival**:
A mark on one Lot that doubles its rent until the next Festival is placed.
_Avoid_: Event tile, boost

**Blackout**:
A mark on one Lot that stops its rent until the turn of the player who placed it comes again.
Players see "Mất điện".
_Avoid_: Outage, freeze

**Planning**:
A Card's free Level on a City of the drawer's. Players see "Quy hoạch".
_Avoid_: Free upgrade, zoning

**Quiz**:
A grade-5 maths problem with fresh numbers and four answers, which the drawer may take on
instead of a safe reward, for a bigger one if answered right in time. Players see "Đố".
_Avoid_: Question, trivia, puzzle

## Losing

**Bankruptcy**:
A player leaving the Match because a Debt cannot be paid; the last player not bankrupt wins.
_Avoid_: Elimination, game over
